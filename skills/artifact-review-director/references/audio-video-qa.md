# Audio and video QA

## Audio QA

- Confirm file opens, duration, sample rate, channels, codec, and requested format.
- Check silence, truncation, clipping, distortion, noise, clicks, abrupt edits, and unintended level changes.
- Check speech intelligibility, pronunciation, pacing, pauses, and requested voice characteristics.
- Confirm music, dialogue, and effects have an appropriate balance.
- Compare transcript or script to spoken content when available.

## Video QA

- Confirm file opens, duration, dimensions, frame rate, codec, audio tracks, and requested format.
- Check black frames, frozen frames, dropped content, visual artifacts, incorrect aspect ratio, unsafe crops, and incomplete opening/ending.
- Check audio/video synchronization, transitions, titles, lower thirds, logos, and subtitle spelling/timing.
- Confirm visible source credits, permissions, and watermarks as required.

## User review

- Audio: provide playable preview, duration, transcript, and timestamped decision points.
- Video: provide a practical preview, keyframes, shot/section list, subtitle sample, and timestamped decision points.

Invite timestamp feedback. Use lower-resolution previews when they materially reduce review cost, but QA the final-resolution output before delivery.
