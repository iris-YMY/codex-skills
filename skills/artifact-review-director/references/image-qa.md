# Image QA

Use the image's owning creation or editing workflow. Inspect the actual final bitmap at intended display size and at full resolution.

## QA

- Confirm file opens, dimensions, aspect ratio, color mode, transparency, and requested format.
- Check blur, compression damage, halos, seams, banding, unintended cropping, and broken transparency.
- Inspect generated hands, faces, text, logos, reflections, repeated objects, geometry, and background edges for artifacts.
- Confirm embedded text spelling, legibility, hierarchy, and safe margins.
- Confirm the subject, focal point, orientation, and negative space match the intended placement.
- Check watermarks, unwanted marks, sensitive content, and requested brand assets.
- Confirm derivative sizes preserve composition and do not distort the source.

## User review

Show the image inline. For alternatives, label them A/B/C. Ask only about subjective direction such as style, crop, mood, subject position, color, and emphasis.

Use region-based feedback such as “upper-right title area” or “move the subject right.” Deliver the final-resolution asset only after approval when review is required.
